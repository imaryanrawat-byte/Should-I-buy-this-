💡 Smart Money Tips
🛟Keep emergency savings

Try to keep money aside for unexpected expenses. Do not spend every rupee you have on one purchase.

⏳Wait 24 hours

If it is a want, wait one day. If you still want it, compare the price before buying.

📋Know where your money goes

Write down your monthly income, important expenses, and savings. It makes buying decisions much easier.

💰Save before spending

Put some money into savings first, then use what is left for regular spending and wants.

🧾Check the total cost

Delivery, accessories, subscriptions, repairs, and other extras can increase the real price.

🔎Compare before buying

Check a few trusted sellers. Look at the final price, warranty, reviews, and return policy.

💳Be careful with EMI

Do not look only at the monthly payment. Check the total amount you will pay over the full EMI period.

⭐Need or want?

Ask yourself: “Will I actually use this regularly?” A simple question can prevent many impulse purchases.
